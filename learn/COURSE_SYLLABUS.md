# Course Syllabus

**Quantum Links: From a Bench to Mars** — a 15-week upper-division or first-year-graduate course built entirely from this repository. Every week has readings (`learn/`), a computation (`simulations/`, `notebooks/`, or an explorer), and, where a bench exists, a laboratory (`experiments/protocols/`). Assessment is by the exercises at the end of each `learn/` file, four laboratory reports, and a final project that extends one proposal in `experiments/proposed/`.

Prerequisites: linear algebra, one semester of quantum mechanics or the willingness to work through `learn/00_foundations/01`–`03` in week 1, and Python.

**Final project** (choose one proposal E1–E10 or theory T01–T10): reproduce its cheap version from the repository, extend one assumption, and report the change in one number. Rubric: physics correct and cited (40 %), computation reproducible from the repository (30 %), honest statement of limits (20 %), presentation (10 %).

**Laboratory budget**: P01 $170–500 (`experiments/bench/P01_bill_of_materials.md`), P02 adds a pulse generator, P03 $5–15k, P04 $1–2k above P03. A course without a budget runs weeks 5–14 on the simulations and the synthetic-data pipelines.

**Instructor notes**: every figure in the readings is generated by a function under test (`scripts/make_figures.py`), so a student who changes a parameter and re-runs sees the physics move; every number in `research/thesis/results/tables.md` is regenerated from code. The `youtube/README.md` list pairs each topic with a verified video in English.
