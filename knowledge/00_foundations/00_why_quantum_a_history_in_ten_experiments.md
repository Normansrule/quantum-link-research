# Why quantum? The ten experiments that forced the theory

Quantum mechanics was not invented; it was forced on physicists by measurements that classical physics could not explain. Reading these ten in order is the fastest way to understand what the postulates in `02_postulates_and_measurement.md` are *for*.

| # | Year | Experiment | Classical expectation | What was seen | Concept born |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1900 | Blackbody spectrum (Planck's fit to Lummer–Pringsheim data) | ultraviolet catastrophe, infinite energy | finite spectrum $\propto\nu^3/(e^{h\nu/k_BT}-1)$ | quantization of energy $E=h\nu$; the same $\bar n$ that limits every link in this repo |
| 2 | 1905 | Photoelectric effect (Lenard; Einstein's explanation; Millikan 1916) | electron energy grows with light intensity | grows with frequency, threshold, intensity only sets count | the photon |
| 3 | 1909 | Rutherford scattering (Geiger–Marsden) | plum pudding, gentle deflection | some alpha particles bounce back | the nucleus; atoms are mostly empty |
| 4 | 1913 | Hydrogen spectral lines (Balmer 1885, Bohr's model 1913) | continuous radiation, atoms collapse | discrete lines $1/\lambda=R(1/n^2-1/m^2)$ | quantized orbits; stationary states |
| 5 | 1922 | Stern–Gerlach | continuous smear of magnetic moments | two discrete spots | spin; the first qubit measurement (`05_experiments/done/01`) |
| 6 | 1923 | Compton scattering | wavelength unchanged | wavelength shift depends on angle | photons carry momentum $p=h/\lambda$ |
| 7 | 1927 | Davisson–Germer electron diffraction | particles do not diffract | Bragg peaks from a nickel crystal | matter waves, $\lambda=h/p$ (de Broglie 1924) |
| 8 | 1935–1982 | EPR argument → Bell → Aspect | correlations have local causes | CHSH $S=2.7>2$ | entanglement is real and nonlocal (`05_entanglement_bell_states.md`) |
| 9 | 1986 | Grangier–Roger–Aspect anticorrelation and interference | a wave splits, a particle does not interfere | one photon does both | complementarity, single-photon optics (`05_experiments/done/02`) |
| 10 | 1987 | Hong–Ou–Mandel | independent particles arrive independently | identical photons always leave together | bosonic exchange symmetry; the basis of every photonic Bell measurement (`05_experiments/done/06`) |

The pattern: each experiment measured a *discrete* or *interference* outcome where the classical picture demanded continuity or independence. The postulates are the smallest set of rules that reproduce all ten.

## Key papers and texts
- Planck, M. (1901). Ueber das Gesetz der Energieverteilung im Normalspectrum. *Annalen der Physik*, 309, 553. https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19013090310
- Einstein, A. (1905). Über einen die Erzeugung und Verwandlung des Lichtes betreffenden heuristischen Gesichtspunkt. *Annalen der Physik*, 322, 132.
- Bohr, N. (1913). On the constitution of atoms and molecules. *Philosophical Magazine*, 26, 1.
- Compton, A. H. (1923). A quantum theory of the scattering of X-rays by light elements. *Physical Review*, 21, 483. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.21.483
- Davisson, C., & Germer, L. H. (1927). Diffraction of electrons by a crystal of nickel. *Physical Review*, 30, 705. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.30.705
- Feynman, R. P., Leighton, R. B., & Sands, M. (1965). *The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. III*, ch. 1–3 (online at feynmanlectures.caltech.edu).
- Baggott, J. (2011). *The Quantum Story: A History in 40 Moments*. Oxford University Press.
