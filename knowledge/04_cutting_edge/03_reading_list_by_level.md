# Reading list by level (English)

## Level 0 — first contact
- Susskind, L., & Friedman, A. (2014). *Quantum Mechanics: The Theoretical Minimum*. Basic Books.
- Mermin, N. D. (2007). *Quantum Computer Science: An Introduction*. Cambridge University Press.
- MIT OpenCourseWare 8.04/8.05 (Zwiebach) lectures; Qiskit Textbook (online).

## Level 1 — the standard text and the classic reviews
- Nielsen, M. A., & Chuang, I. L. (2010). *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*.
- Preskill, J. Ph219 lecture notes (Caltech, online).
- Ladd, T. D., et al. (2010). Quantum computers. *Nature*, 464, 45.
- Preskill, J. (2018). Quantum computing in the NISQ era and beyond. *Quantum*, 2, 79.

## Level 2 — one review per modality
- Krantz et al. 2019 (superconducting); Blais et al. 2021 (circuit QED); Burkard et al. 2023 (spins); Doherty et al. 2013 (NV); Bruzewicz et al. 2019 (ions); Saffman et al. 2010 and Henriet et al. 2020 (atoms); Flamini et al. 2019 and Kok et al. 2007 (photonics); Nayak et al. 2008 (topological).

## Level 3 — communication
- Gisin, N., Ribordy, G., Tittel, W., & Zbinden, H. (2002). Quantum cryptography. *Rev. Mod. Phys.*, 74, 145.
- Scarani et al. 2009; Xu et al. 2020; Pirandola et al. 2020 (QKD security and practice).
- Sangouard et al. 2011; Azuma et al. 2023 (repeaters). Lvovsky et al. 2009 (memories).
- Wehner, S., Elkouss, D., & Hanson, R. (2018). Quantum internet: a vision for the road ahead. *Science*, 362, eaam9288.
- Bedington et al. 2017; Sidhu et al. 2021 (space).

## Level 4 — where the field is now
- Google Quantum AI 2023, 2025 (surface code); Bluvstein et al. 2024 (logical atoms); Knaut et al. 2024 and Stolk et al. 2024 (metropolitan networks); Li et al. 2025 (microsatellite QKD); Wang et al. 2025 (13-hour memory); Mohageg et al. 2022, 2025 (deep-space link).

## Video courses (English)
- MIT 8.04–8.06 (OCW); Umesh Vazirani, Quantum Mechanics and Quantum Computation (Berkeley, edX archive); Qiskit Global Summer School recordings; QuTech Academy "Quantum Internet" (edX).
