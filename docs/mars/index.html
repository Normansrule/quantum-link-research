Mars link simulator

The Earth–Mars link, day by day

Kepler mean-element orbits (the same model as qll/space/ephemeris.py, ported line by line and checked to 10⁻¹⁵), solar-conjunction blackout at a Sun–Earth–Mars angle below the threshold, optional relays at Sun–Earth L4/L5, and the demonstrated memories that would outlive today's round trip. Drag the date or press play.

Model limits: coplanar mean elements (range good to ~1 %), a geometric blackout criterion for the direct path (relay legs are drawn but not checked against the Sun here; qll.space.relay_constellation checks them), and memory lifetimes from the repository's table at f₀ = 0.95. See qll/space.
