Link budget · flagship F3 · REQ-CAP-001

A trillion pairs a second leave Earth. How many reach Mars?

Every stage of the link multiplies the rate by a factor from a tested model: optics, the atmosphere, the spreading of the beam over hundreds of millions of kilometres, pointing jitter, detectors, memories, and the Sun getting in the way. Then the pair must wait in a memory for Earth's two classical bits. The stream below is drawn from qll/systems/mars_budget.py, ported line by line to docs/js/budget_core.js and checked against it.

A memory is paid for twice here: its retrieval efficiency at both ends multiplies the rate, and its coherence time sets the fidelity after the wait. Assumptions (all adjustable in MarsLinkDesign): 1550 nm, transmit optics 0.8, receive optics 0.7, detectors 0.9, Earth elevation 40°, weather and operations duty 0.5, storage for one round trip, depolarizing memories.
