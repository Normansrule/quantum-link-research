# Technology readiness (NASA scale)

The levels are defined in `qll/systems/trl.py` [nasa2016seh]. Assessment for the elements of the baseline:

| Element | TRL | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Fiber QKD | 9 | commercial networks |
| Low-Earth-orbit satellite QKD | 7–8 | Micius (2017), Jinan-1 (2025) |
| Measurement-device-independent QKD | 6 | field trials |
| Entanglement swapping between memories | 4 | laboratory networks (Delft, Boston) |
| Minutes-to-hours quantum memory with high retrieval | 3 | trapped-ion hour-class coherence; retrieval into photons not yet at link level |
| Deep-space optical communication (classical channel) | 6–7 | NASA DSOC on Psyche (2023–2025) |
| Microwave–optical transduction | 2 | added noise above efficiency (TS-5) |
| Interplanetary entanglement link | 1–2 | this work: principles and budget |
