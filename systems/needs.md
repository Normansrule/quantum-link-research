# Stakeholder needs

| ID | Need | Stakeholder | Measure of effectiveness |
|---|---|---|---|
| N-1 | Confidential messaging between Earth and a Mars asset whose security does not rest on computational assumptions alone | mission operators | useful entangled pairs and secret bits delivered per day; the messenger never sends unkeyed (REQ-APP-001, REQ-CAP-003) |
| N-2 | A quantitative account of what temperature, loss, and light time do to every link in the chain | system designers | every stage of the budget computed by a tested model with a cited source (REQ-PHY, THM, CHN, SPC) |
| N-3 | An honest separation of what is achievable today from what is open research | the thesis committee, funders | each claim marked demonstrated, modelled, or open; rate never reported without the fidelity it comes with (REQ-NET-002) |
| N-4 | Reproducible, citable models suitable for a master's thesis and for teaching | students, instructors | every number regenerated from code on each commit; tests pass on two operating systems (REQ-SYS, REQ-WEB-001) |
| N-5 | An open-source artifact others can extend | the research community | one idea per file, documented interfaces, MIT licence |

Requirements trace to these needs in `traceability_matrix.csv` (column `need`); `requirements.md` groups them by need.
