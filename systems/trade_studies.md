# Trade studies

Each trade names the alternatives, the criterion, the result computed by tested code, and the decision. The result tables are regenerated by `scripts/build_systems_docs.py` from `qll/systems/mars_budget.py` and `qll/network/purified_chain.py` on every commit (the pre-commit hook and CI run it, and `tests/test_systems_docs.py` fails if they drift). Baseline design unless stated: 10¹² pairs/s across 1000 modes, 1550 nm, 0.5 m transmit waist, 4 m receiver, 100 nrad pointing, trapped-ion memories, storage for one round trip (learn 03/21).

## TS-1 Link architecture: where the pair is made
**Alternatives.** A pair source at the Earth station, one photon stored locally and one sent to Mars; or a source on a Sun–Earth L4 relay sending one photon to each planet, the Micius two-downlink pattern. **Criterion.** Useful pairs per day at closest and farthest approach (REQ-CAP-003, REQ-CAP-004).

<!-- trade:architecture:start -->
| architecture | pairs/day, closest | pairs/day, farthest | loss at closest |
|---|---|---|---|
| source at Earth (baseline) | 2.9e+07 | 8.7e+05 | -95 dB |
| relay at L4, two downlinks | 0.0007 | 0.00027 | -201 dB |
<!-- trade:architecture:end -->

**Decision.** Source at Earth. Diffraction over an astronomical distance costs about 87 dB; the dual-downlink relay pays it twice. The L4 relay keeps its value as a store-and-forward node during the three conjunction weeks of each synodic period (REQ-SPC-002), which is where it enters the concept of operations.

## TS-2 Carrier wavelength
**Alternatives.** 810 nm (silicon detectors, smaller diffraction) or 1550 nm (telecom components, lower atmospheric loss and daylight background). **Criterion.** Useful pairs per day at the farthest point, all other stages unchanged.

<!-- trade:wavelength:start -->
| wavelength | pairs/day, farthest | diffraction factor | atmosphere factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 810 nm | 2.1e+06 | 1.96e-10 | 0.58 |
| 1550 nm | 8.7e+05 | 5.34e-11 | 0.79 |
<!-- trade:wavelength:end -->

**Decision.** Open. Diffraction scales as λ², so 810 nm wins in this budget by the factor above, but the budget does not yet charge 810 nm for its higher daylight background or credit 1550 nm for fiber-compatible memories and frequency conversion (learn 03/05). Resolving it needs the background model of `qll/channels/thermal_background.py` in the budget: backlog item for the thesis.

## TS-3 Memory platform
**Alternatives.** The six demonstrated memories in `MEMORY_TABLE`. **Criteria.** Pairs per day and fidelity to Mars at the farthest point (retrieval efficiency paid at both ends, coherence against a 44-minute wait), and the fiber distances over which the memory makes a useful repeater chain that beats direct transmission (learn 03/20).

<!-- trade:memory:start -->
| memory | coherence | retrieval | pairs/day to Mars, farthest | fidelity after storage | useful fiber-repeater range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rb/Cs atomic ensemble (DLCZ) | 0.001 s | 50 % | 2.2e+05 | 0.50 | none |
| SiV nuclear spin in cavity | 1 s | 90 % | 7.2e+05 | 0.50 | none |
| NV 13C register | 60 s | 90 % | 7.2e+05 | 0.50 | 409–897 km |
| 171Yb+ hyperfine | 3600 s | 99 % | 8.7e+05 | 0.73 | 389–2242 km |
| Eu:YSO nuclear spin, 6 h (ZEFOZ+DD) | 21600 s | 1 % | 88 | 0.91 | none |
| Eu:YSO nuclear spin, 13.1 h | 47160 s | 0.5 % | 22 | 0.94 | none |
<!-- trade:memory:end -->

**Decision.** Trapped-ion (¹⁷¹Yb⁺) memories as the baseline: the only platform both fast and useful at every point of the orbit and in fiber chains. Rare-earth crystals are the high-fidelity, low-rate alternative for the Mars end if their retrieval efficiency rises; nitrogen-vacancy registers qualify for terrestrial chains only. This refines trade E8.

## TS-4 Apertures
**Alternatives.** Mars receivers from 1 to 16 m and transmit waists from 0.15 to 1.5 m. **Criterion.** Useful pairs per day at the farthest point.

<!-- trade:aperture:start -->
| Mars receiver | transmit waist 0.15 m | 0.5 m (baseline) | 1.5 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 m | 5.1e+03 | 5.4e+04 | 3.7e+05 |
| 2 m | 2.0e+04 | 2.2e+05 | 1.5e+06 |
| 4 m | 8.1e+04 | 8.7e+05 | 5.9e+06 |
| 8 m | 3.2e+05 | 3.5e+06 | 2.4e+07 |
| 16 m | 1.3e+06 | 1.4e+07 | 9.5e+07 |
<!-- trade:aperture:end -->

**Decision.** In the far field the rate scales as (transmit waist × receiver diameter)², so the two trade against each other one for one; mass and pointing (100 nrad is already demanding) decide the split. The baseline 0.5 m waist and 4 m receiver clear REQ-CAP-003 with margin of about 9 at the farthest point.

## TS-5 Transduction
Result (0.15.0, S07): transduction-free architecture chosen as baseline; a transducer becomes competitive only when its added noise is well below its efficiency (n_add ≪ η_t), which no verified device reaches at the time of writing.
