# Trade studies
1. Relay placement (Earth orbit, cislunar, Mars orbit, mid-path).
2. Carrier wavelength (637/810 nm vs 1310/1550 nm) vs. detector, background, and diffraction.
3. Memory platform vs. operating temperature and coherence time.
4. Aperture diameter vs. mass and pointing.

Result (0.15.0, S07): transduction-free architecture chosen as baseline; a transducer becomes competitive only when its added noise is well below its efficiency (n_add ≪ η_t), which no verified device reaches at the time of writing.
